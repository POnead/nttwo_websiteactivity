About CafeTrack

CafeTrack is a connected inventory management system built for Merrylane Cafe Foodhub. It replaces manual stock tracking with a QR-based workflow that eliminates guesswork, prevents spoilage, and keeps every ingredient accounted for.

The system is designed to solve three core problems: ingredient waste from missed expiration dates, stockouts from untracked usage, and slow checkout during peak hours.

Ingredient Waste

Expired items go unnoticed until it's too late.

Stockouts

No visibility on what's running low until it's gone.

Slow Checkout

Manual logging takes time during busy hours.

No Accountability

Hard to trace who took what and when.

How It Works

Four simple steps to keep your inventory accurate.

1
Register Ingredient

Admin adds item details and expiration date. System generates a unique QR code.

2
Scan Personal QR

Staff logs in via personal QR badge plus password. Two-factor auth built in.

3
Scan Item to Check In/Out

Scan the item's QR code to deduct or add stock. Multi-item checkout supported.

4
Dashboard Updates Live

Admin sees real-time stock levels and alerts for low-stock or expiring items.

Features

Real-Time Tracking

Monitor stock levels, expiration dates, and usage trends in live dashboards.

QR Code Scanning

Staff scan their personal QR to log in, then scan item codes to check stock in or out in seconds.

Smart Alerts

Get notified when stock runs low or ingredients approach their expiration date.

Role-Based Access

Admins manage items and reports. Staff can only check in/out stock. Clear separation of duties.

Audit Trail

Every transaction is logged with staff ID and timestamp. Records cannot be edited or deleted.

Usage Reports

Generate usage and waste reports. Filter by date, category, or staff member.
